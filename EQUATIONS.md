# Aircraft Performance Equations

## 1. Lift Force

**Formula**

L = 0.5 × ρ × V² × S × CL

Where:
- L = Lift Force (N)
- ρ = Air Density (kg/m³)
- V = Velocity (m/s)
- S = Wing Area (m²)
- CL = Lift Coefficient

---

## 2. Drag Force

**Formula**

D = 0.5 × ρ × V² × S × CD

---

## 3. Stall Speed

**Formula**

Vs = √[(2 × W) / (ρ × S × CLmax)]

---

## 4. Lift-to-Drag Ratio

**Formula**

L/D = CL / CD

---

## 5. Dynamic Pressure

**Formula**

q = 0.5 × ρ × V²

---

## 6. Mach Number

**Formula**

M = V / a

---

## 7. Rate of Climb

**Formula**

ROC = (Power Available − Power Required) / Weight

---

## 8. Wing Loading

**Formula**

Wing Loading = W / S

---

## 9. Power Loading

**Formula**

Power Loading = W / P

---

## 10. Aircraft Range

**Formula**

Range = (V / SFC) × (L/D) × ln(Wi / Wf)

---

## 11. Aircraft Endurance

**Formula**

Endurance = (1 / SFC) × (L/D) × ln(Wi / Wf)
